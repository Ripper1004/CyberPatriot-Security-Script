> [!NOTE]
> This toolkit is for **practice and learning**. Under the CyberPatriot 19 rules, AI-assisted scripts may not be used in competition rounds, and scripts made for CyberPatriot may not be posted publicly. If your school competes again, use this to learn, then build your own team materials, and keep them private.
